---
id: FCT-0008
title: Domain facts about external environments and operational limits decay over time
status: active
evidence: Requirements engineering studies (Zave and Jackson 1997; Lehman's laws of software evolution) and production incident post-mortems show that domain properties regarding external APIs, traffic patterns, and regulations become stale as the environment evolves unless scheduled for periodic review.
---

## Statement
Facts that describe properties of the surrounding world, external systems, or operational thresholds lose validity over time and require scheduled review dates so decaying grounds are flagged before dependent requirements silently break.
