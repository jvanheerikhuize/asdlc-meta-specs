## Question
REQ-0004 makes scenarios mandatory, but what is the minimum baseline? We started with the hypothesis ASM-0002 and promoted the bounded baseline observation FCT-0005: every active requirement must supply at least one concrete Given/When/Then scenario to anchor implementation and test traceability, while complex requirements should add boundary/negative scenarios or open questions (`QST`) when ambiguity remains.
