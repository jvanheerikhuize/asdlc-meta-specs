## Question
REQ-0004 makes scenarios mandatory, but how many? We had no number at all. The answer was originally the assumption ASM-0002, which was validated in cross-agent testing and promoted to FCT-0005: at least one Given/When/Then scenario is sufficient to prevent clarification rounds.
